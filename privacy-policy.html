About this Policy

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Abrax Protocol ("the App", "we", "us"), developed by Farizad Studio, handles information when you play. The App is distributed through the Google Play Store. By installing or playing the App, please review the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information ourselves.

Gameplay data, settings, and progression are stored locally on your device using app storage, localStorage, or IndexedDB. We do not operate an account, login, analytics, or developer-controlled cloud-save service.

Third-Party Services

This App uses Google AdMob to provide interstitial and rewarded ads. Depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and Google's configuration, Google may process device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location derived from IP to deliver, measure, prevent fraud in, and improve ads.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Other than AdMob, the App does not include an analytics SDK, account system, or cloud-save service.

Advertising

Rewarded ads are optional and are shown only when you request an in-game reward. The App may also show interstitial ads between game sessions when that feature is enabled. Ad availability and frequency may change in later releases; this policy will be updated if the data practices change.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App's third-party services, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Permissions

The App may request the following Android permissions:

  • Internet — required for serving advertisements.

The App does not request broad access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not collect data on our own servers, there is no server-side data to retain or delete. Primary game data is stored in the App's local storage and is removed when you clear the App's storage. Uninstalling normally removes that local copy. Android backup or device-transfer services may separately retain or restore app data according to your device and backup-provider settings; those services are governed by the provider's own privacy policy.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example to reflect new features or new third-party services. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
