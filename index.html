The studio

Small team energy. (Actually currently only one developer.) Big-screen ambition.

Farizad Studio builds games with cinematic sci-fi and political/history themes in hope of creating a unique and memorable experience for players. The studio is currently a one-person operation, but the goal is to grow into a small team of passionate developers who can bring even more ambitious projects to life.
