Privacy Policy

Abrax Protocol · Last updated: September 5, 2026

About this Policy

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Abrax Protocol ("the App", "we", "us"), developed by Faraz Studio, handles information when you play. The App is distributed through the Google Play Store. By installing or playing the App, please review the practices described here.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Gameplay data, settings, progression, custom music, and custom arena images remain on your device using app storage, localStorage, or IndexedDB. We do not operate an account, login, analytics, or developer-controlled cloud-save service.

Third-Party Services

The App does not include third-party advertising, analytics, account, or cloud-save SDKs. External social-media links open in your browser and are governed by the destination service's own privacy policy.

Advertising

The App contains no advertisements and does not use an advertising SDK.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a privacy concern, please contact us.

Permissions

The App does not request network access or broad access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location. If you choose custom music or a custom arena background, Android's system file picker lets you select that specific file. The selected file is processed locally and is not uploaded to us.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not collect data on our own servers, there is no server-side data to retain or delete. Primary game data is stored in the App's local storage and is removed when you clear the App's storage. Uninstalling normally removes that local copy. Android backup or device-transfer services may separately retain or restore app data according to your device and backup-provider settings; those services are governed by the provider's own privacy policy.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example to reflect new features or new third-party services. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
